Delete your DE Interview Prep account

The quickest option is available inside the app:

  1. Sign in to DE Interview Prep.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account and confirm.

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your account. Use the subject “Account deletion request.”

Request account deletion

Deleting your account permanently removes your account and associated custom content, quiz history, and saved progress. We may contact you to verify ownership before completing an email request.
