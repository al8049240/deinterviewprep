Privacy Policy for DE Interview Prep

Effective date: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DE Interview Prep ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the DE Interview Prep mobile application (the "App").

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and account user ID when you choose to create an account or sign in, including through Google Sign-In.
  • Learning activity: quiz attempts, selected answers, scores, accuracy, topics studied, study duration, and saved progress for signed-in users.
  • Content you create: custom flashcards, case scenarios, developer experiences, ratings, reviews, and written feedback that you choose to submit. Reviews may be published after moderation and may then be visible to other users.
  • Purchase information: product identifiers, purchase status, and entitlement information needed to unlock or restore paid features. Google Play processes your payment method; we do not receive your full card, bank-account, or other payment credentials.

You may use available guest features without creating an account. Information stored only on your device, such as some preferences and locally cached content, is not sent to us unless a feature explicitly synchronizes it with your account.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • create, authenticate, secure, and manage user accounts;
  • provide quizzes, custom study content, bookmarks, and other App features;
  • synchronize progress and show personalized statistics and weak-topic insights;
  • verify, unlock, and restore purchases;
  • send account verification, password-reset, security, and support communications;
  • review feedback, moderate submitted reviews, prevent abuse, and maintain App security;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

Service providers and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers only as needed to operate the App, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database hosting, synchronization, and account deletion;
  • Google Sign-In when you choose to authenticate with your Google account;
  • Google Play Billing to process purchases and restore entitlements.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business reorganization, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Advertising and analytics

The current version of the App does not use third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. If this changes, we will update this Privacy Policy and the App's Google Play Data Safety disclosures before the relevant version is released, and we will provide any consent choices required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We retain account information and synchronized App data while your account remains active or as needed to provide the App. We may retain limited information for longer when required for security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or enforcement. Google Play retains transaction records according to Google's own policies.

You can permanently delete your account and associated data by:

  1. signing in to the App;
  2. opening Settings;
  3. selecting Delete Account and confirming.

If you cannot access the App, follow the instructions on our account deletion page or email centralmail111@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We may verify account ownership before completing an email request.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted network connections and access controls, to protect personal information. No electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate legal safeguards for international data transfers.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information. You can update your display name in the App and delete your account as described above. To make another privacy request, contact us using the email address below.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults preparing for professional interviews and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so that we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App or applicable requirements change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the App or through another appropriate channel.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email centralmail111@gmail.com.
